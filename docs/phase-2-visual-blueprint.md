# KS Lawn Ranger — Phase 2 Visual Blueprint

## Status
Phase 2 establishes the final page structure, image assignments, visual hierarchy, and content flow before implementation. It uses the completed Phase 1 media library as the source of truth.

## Production direction
- Premium local service brand, not a generic landscaping template.
- Maroon, black, white, and warm neutral palette.
- Real project photography appears early and repeatedly.
- Strong mobile experience with call and estimate actions always easy to reach.
- Clear separation between residential services, commercial maintenance, seasonal work, and snow/ice.
- Copy stays direct, local, and specific to KS Lawn Ranger.

## Page-by-page visual plan

### Home
- Image-led hero with one clear promise and two actions.
- Four-item proof strip immediately after hero.
- Featured work before long service copy.
- About section uses text first; no weak portrait should be forced into the design.
- Four service blocks each receive one real project image on desktop and compact image treatment on mobile.
- One interactive before/after slider only, to preserve performance and impact.
- Commercial section uses a darker, more operational visual style.
- Estimate form remains the final dominant conversion section.

### Services page
- Opening banner uses alternate hero image.
- Each service group uses one strong image and concise scope copy.
- Before/after pairs are placed beside the most relevant service:
  - Landscaping: BA-02, BA-03, BA-04
  - Cleanup: BA-01, BA-06
  - Pruning: BA-05
  - Rock/bed work: BA-07
- Snow section uses the action image plus a compact three-image strip.

### Commercial page
- Hero: commercial mowing image.
- Supporting visual: commercial rock installation.
- Winter section: larger cleared lot/drive image.
- Avoid residential imagery except where showing service capability.
- Include property types and maintenance scope in structured blocks.

### Gallery page
- Masonry-style visual grid with consistent spacing.
- Use only A and B images.
- Do not show near-duplicates beside one another.
- Place before/after pairs in dedicated comparison blocks rather than normal gallery tiles.
- Lazy-load all below-the-fold images.

### About page
- Lead with company story and standards.
- Use work/equipment imagery only if no suitable professional owner/team photo exists.
- Do not use a weak personal photo simply to fill space.

### Contact page
- Minimal imagery.
- Small supporting lawn image or subtle background only.
- Prioritize phone, text, email, service area, hours, and estimate form.

## Responsive image rules
- Generate desktop and mobile crops for hero and major banner images.
- Use WebP or AVIF where supported, with JPEG/PNG fallback as needed.
- Hero target: roughly 180–300 KB after optimization.
- Card images: roughly 80–180 KB each.
- Gallery thumbnails: roughly 60–140 KB each.
- Preserve original files outside the production image directory.
- Add descriptive alt text based on the visible service and outcome, not keyword stuffing.

## Content and trust rules
- Replace all placeholder reviews before launch.
- Do not display a review count or rating unless verified from the live Google Business profile.
- Keep “fully insured,” “locally owned,” and service-area claims consistent throughout.
- Use Monday–Saturday, 7 AM–6 PM consistently.
- Keep phone number 913-221-7981 and business email consistent.
- Avoid unsupported claims such as “best,” “number one,” or guaranteed response times unless operationally true.
