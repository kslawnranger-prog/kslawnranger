12 Frequently asked

Clear answers.
No guesswork.

Are you fully insured?

Yes. KS Lawn Ranger LLC is fully insured for the property services we provide.

Do I need to be home during service?

Usually not. As long as we have access to the work area and any needed instructions, most services can be completed without you being home.

What happens when weather delays mowing?

We adjust the route as conditions allow and communicate when weather materially changes the expected service day.

Do you handle commercial properties?

Yes. We serve apartments, retail spaces, offices, associations, churches, storage facilities, and other managed properties.

How quickly will you respond to an estimate request?

Most requests receive a response within one business day. Larger or more detailed projects may require an on-site visit before pricing.

Do you offer one-time projects as well as recurring care?

Yes. Services range from recurring mowing and property maintenance to mulch, rock, sod, drainage, cleanup, and seasonal projects.
