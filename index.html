09 Frequently asked

Clear answers.
No guesswork.

Are you fully insured?

Yes. KS Lawn Ranger LLC is fully insured for the property services we provide.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. As long as we have access to the work area and any necessary instructions, most services can be completed without you being home.

What happens when weather delays service?

We adjust the schedule as conditions allow and communicate when weather materially changes the expected service day.

Do you offer commercial maintenance?

Yes. We maintain apartments, retail sites, offices, churches, associations, storage facilities, and other managed properties.

How do estimates work?

Send the form below with your property details. We will review the request, ask any needed questions, and arrange a site visit when appropriate.
